# User guide: five minutes to a board memo

1. **Pick a business** from the six cards at the top. Each card shows how many of its missions you have completed.
2. **Read the baseline.** The context line shows baseline EBITDA and EV. With nothing moved, the *Board Memo* tab lists the three levers to explore first.
3. **Move levers** on the left.
   - The gold tick marks the baseline. Double-click a slider to reset it.
   - A **green** span means the move creates value; **red** means it destroys value.
   - Each moved lever shows its one-off **investment**. Hover the ⓘ to see the trade-off built into the model.
   - Or start from a **scenario preset** chip (for example, *Utilisation drive*, *Softer market*).
4. **Read the Value Simulator tab.**
   - The tiles show EBITDA, margin, EV, cash released, value created and investment against the budget envelope.
   - The **EBITDA bridge** shows which lever did the work.
   - The **tornado** shows which drivers matter most from where you are now.
   - **Where to play next** ranks the next 10% move on every lever. Press **Play** to apply it.
5. **Watch the right-hand panel.** Risk flags lower **board confidence**, and your **value score** is value × confidence. Complete **missions** to earn XP, and climb from Analyst to Chief Value Officer.
6. **Check the Balanced Scorecard.** Cost cuts that hurt customers turn the Customer perspective amber or red.
7. **Check the Business Case.** It shows NPV, IRR and payback after UAE corporate tax, the year-by-year benefit ramp, and an initiative register with owners.
8. **Export the Board Memo** with *Copy Markdown*, *Download .md* or *Print / PDF*.
9. **Share** the scenario. The URL carries every lever, so a colleague opens exactly what you built.

**Tip:** the fastest route to a high score is rarely the biggest slider move. Look for levers with short payback that raise no flags, and use the working-capital levers. Cash counts as value.
