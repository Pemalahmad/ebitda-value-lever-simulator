# User guide: from market signal to a board memo

1. **Start with the Market Tracker.** Read the official-data snapshot date, headline transaction flows, leading communities and market signals. Every published figure links to its source.
2. **Connect deal-level data when needed.** Ingest a DLD Transactions CSV to add monthly deal flow, median AED/m², off-plan share, community momentum and top projects.
3. **Open Value Simulator** and pick one of the six market-facing operating models. Each model card shows how many of its missions you have completed.
4. **Read the baseline.** The context line shows baseline EBITDA and EV. With nothing moved, the *Board Memo* tab lists the three levers to explore first.
5. **Move levers** on the left.
   - The gold tick marks the baseline. Double-click a slider to reset it.
   - A **green** span means the move creates value; **red** means it destroys value.
   - Each moved lever shows its one-off **investment**. Hover the ⓘ to see the trade-off built into the model.
   - Or start from a **scenario preset** chip (for example, *Utilisation drive*, *Softer market*).
6. **Read the Value Simulator tab.**
   - The tiles show EBITDA, margin, EV, cash released, value created and investment against the budget envelope.
   - The **EBITDA bridge** shows which lever did the work.
   - The **tornado** shows which drivers matter most from where you are now.
   - **Where to play next** ranks the next 10% move on every lever. Press **Play** to apply it.
7. **Watch the right-hand panel.** Risk flags lower **board confidence**, and your **value score** is value × confidence. Complete **missions** to earn XP, and climb from Analyst to Chief Value Officer.
8. **Check the Balanced Scorecard.** Cost cuts that hurt customers turn the Customer perspective amber or red.
9. **Check the Business Case.** It shows NPV, IRR and payback after UAE corporate tax, the year-by-year benefit ramp, and an initiative register with owners.
10. **Use Portfolio Lab** to combine sector scenarios and test transparent portfolio-level plays without tying the analysis to a named company.
11. **Export the Board Memo** with *Copy Markdown*, *Download .md* or *Print / PDF*.
12. **Share** the scenario. The URL carries every lever, so a colleague opens exactly what you built.

**Tip:** use the tracker to frame the market question first, then test only the levers that answer it. The fastest route to a high score is rarely the biggest slider move: look for short payback, manageable risks and working-capital improvements.
