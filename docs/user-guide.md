# User guide: from market signal to a board memo

1. **Start with the Market Tracker.** Read the official-data snapshot date and use the headline switch to morph the main instrument between value, transaction volume and investors. Every published figure links to its source. Use the header switch to choose light or dark mode; the browser remembers your choice.
2. **Use the evidence calendar.** Move between months and select marked days to inspect publication checks, model refreshes, decision reviews and memo cut-offs. Calendar entries describe an internal review cadence unless they explicitly identify a published source.
3. **Interrogate the history chart.** Switch between value, transactions and investors, then select a period to inspect its exact value, comparison basis, reporting scope and source. Partial periods are not treated as full-year comparisons.
4. **Choose a working case.** Use the scenario deck or arrow keys to select Base, Upside, Growth, Downside or Liquidity Stress. Open the comparison card to place the selected illustrative case beside the published market level. These cases frame analysis; they are not forecasts.
5. **Run the market-signal SOP.** Work through *Validate evidence*, *Classify materiality*, *Set the response* and *Record and review*. Each gate identifies its owner, service level, required evidence and output. Progress and the selected response are saved locally.
6. **Connect deal-level data when needed.** Choose a DLD Transactions CSV in the import lab. The browser validates required fields, counts rejected rows and builds the deal-level workspace locally without uploading the file. Use the CLI ingest route when the aggregate should ship with the build.
7. **Use the mobile command dock.** On a narrow screen, jump directly to the pulse, charts, cases, SOP or import lab.
8. **Open Value Simulator** and pick one of the six market-facing operating models. Each model card shows how many of its missions you have completed.
9. **Read the baseline.** The context line shows baseline EBITDA and EV. With nothing moved, the *Board Memo* tab lists the three levers to explore first.
10. **Move levers** on the left.
   - The blue tick marks the baseline. Double-click a slider to reset it.
   - A **green** span means the move creates value; **red** means it destroys value.
   - Each moved lever shows its one-off **investment**. Hover the ⓘ to see the trade-off built into the model.
   - Or start from a **scenario preset** chip (for example, *Utilisation drive*, *Softer market*).
11. **Read the Value Simulator tab.**
   - The tiles show EBITDA, margin, EV, cash released, value created and investment against the budget envelope.
   - The **EBITDA bridge** shows which lever did the work.
   - The **tornado** shows which drivers matter most from where you are now.
   - **Where to play next** ranks the next 10% move on every lever. Press **Play** to apply it.
12. **Watch the right-hand panel.** Risk flags lower **board confidence**, and your **value score** is value × confidence. Complete **missions** to earn XP, and climb from Analyst to Chief Value Officer.
13. **Check the Balanced Scorecard.** Cost cuts that hurt customers turn the Customer perspective amber or red.
14. **Check the Business Case.** It shows NPV, IRR and payback after UAE corporate tax, the year-by-year benefit ramp, and an initiative register with owners.
15. **Use Portfolio Lab** to combine sector scenarios and test transparent portfolio-level plays without tying the analysis to a named company.
16. **Export the Board Memo** with *Copy Markdown*, *Download .md* or *Print / PDF*.
17. **Share** the scenario. The URL carries every lever, so a colleague opens exactly what you built.

**Tip:** use the tracker to frame the market question first, then test only the levers that answer it. The fastest route to a high score is rarely the biggest slider move: look for short payback, manageable risks and working-capital improvements.
